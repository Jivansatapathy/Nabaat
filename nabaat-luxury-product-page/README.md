# NABAAT Store - Luxury D2C Product Page UX

Transform every WooCommerce product on your store into an ultra-premium, high-converting Direct-To-Consumer (D2C) product experience.

## Features
- **Editorial Brand Aesthetics**: Custom typography pairing (`Playfair Display` serif headings + `Plus Jakarta Sans`), curated luxury color palettes, and polished micro-interactions.
- **Interactive Product Gallery**: Floating bestseller/monsoon-proof badges, 360° rotation preview, hover zoom & pan, and a 4-thumbnail grid.
- **4-Column Heritage Trust Strip**: Guaranteed hardware & seam, monsoon/weather tested, airline cabin approved, handcrafted heritage.
- **Dynamic Pricing & Tax Card**: Highlighting sale price, MRP strikethrough, discount pill, GST inclusive note, and free express delivery.
- **Interactive Colorway Swatches**: Visual color pills with active checkmarks and dynamic variant labeling.
- **Sizing Mode & Blueprint**: Standard Dimensions vs Bespoke/Custom Fit toggle with selectable size cards and domestic airline overhead statement.
- **Pincode Delivery Estimator**: Real-time 6-digit Indian PIN verification with estimated arrival date, city detection, and COD eligibility badge.
- **High-Converting CTAs**: Stepper quantity box (`[-] 1 [+]`), Primary *"Add to Cart"* button, and Gold *"Buy Now — Instant Checkout"* button.
- **Direct WhatsApp Specialist Consultation**: Pre-configured WhatsApp consultation bar with pre-filled message.
- **Modern Accordion Sections**: Structured specifications, weather-proof tech, express delivery/returns, and customer review scores.
- **Mobile Sticky Purchase Bar**: Boosts mobile conversions with an always-accessible purchase bar while scrolling.

## Installation
1. Go to **WordPress Admin > Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin**.
2. Select `nabaat-luxury-product-page.zip`.
3. Click **Install Now** and **Activate Plugin**.
4. Every single product across your store will instantly adopt the luxury D2C layout!
5. To configure WhatsApp numbers or toggles, visit **WooCommerce > Luxury Product UX**.
